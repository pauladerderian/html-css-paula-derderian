Our Commitment to the Environment

← Back to Blog

We are excited to introduce a recycling initiative where customers can return their worn jackets to us. By rethinking materials and extending product life cycles, we aim to help shape the jacket of the future.

Every jacket we sell eventually reaches the end of its life, whether through years of hard wear or simply because a customer has outgrown it. Rather than letting that be the end of the story, our take-back program lets you send any Rainy Days jacket back to us, in any condition, so it can be sorted, broken down, and given a second life instead of ending up in a landfill.

Jackets that are still in good shape are cleaned, repaired where needed, and resold through our upcoming refurbished line. Jackets that are too worn to reuse are broken down for their raw materials: the outer shells are recycled into new fabric, the insulation is reprocessed, and even the hardware, zippers, snaps, and buckles, is sorted and reused wherever possible.

This initiative is part of a bigger shift in how we design. We're moving toward fabrics made from recycled and bio-based sources, cutting down on the chemical treatments used in waterproofing, and designing jackets to be taken apart and repaired rather than thrown away. Every new style we release is measured against the last one on these standards, not just on how it looks or performs.

We know this is a long-term project, not a single campaign, and we'll keep sharing our progress here as the take-back program grows. If you have an old jacket sitting in the back of your closet, we'd love to give it a second life; details on how to send it in will be posted here soon.
