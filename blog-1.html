Built to Endure

← Back to Blog

We proudly present our newest jacket, built to last, Endurance. It has been a long journey to reach this point, but for us, the path is part of the reward. Our team has dedicated itself fully to developing outerwear that is comfortable, adjustable, and above all, sustainable. In recent years, we've taken meaningful steps towards becoming more environmentally responsible and Endurance represents a major milestone in that commitment.

Every Endurance jacket starts with the same question we ask about all our outerwear: how long can this actually last? Instead of designing around a season or a trend, our team spent over a year testing prototypes in the wettest, windiest conditions we could find, from coastal storms to mountain trails, refining the fit and the fabric until both held up to daily wear without losing shape or waterproofing.

The result is a shell built from a recycled, high-density weave that resists abrasion far better than the materials we've used before, paired with fully adjustable cuffs, hem, and hood so the jacket can be fitted to the moment, whether that's layering up for a cold commute or loosening up for a long hike. Every seam is taped and pressure-tested, and the zippers are rated for years of daily use, not just a single wet season.

Sustainability shaped this jacket from the very first sketch. The outer shell uses recycled polyester sourced from post-consumer waste, the insulation is PFC-free, and the dyes used throughout meet strict environmental standards for water use and runoff. We also designed Endurance to be repairable: loose seams, popped snaps, and worn zippers can all be serviced through our care program rather than becoming a reason to replace the jacket entirely.

Endurance is available now in our full range of sizes, in three colourways designed to work across seasons. As always, we'd love to hear how it holds up for you, so keep an eye on our blog for updates from customers putting it through its paces.
