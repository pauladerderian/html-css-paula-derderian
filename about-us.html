About Us

Rainy Days started with a simple frustration: most outdoor jackets were either built to perform or built to look good, rarely both. We set out to make outerwear that holds up to real weather without asking you to compromise on how it fits into your everyday life.

We're a small team based in Norway, which means we don't have to imagine what our jackets need to survive, we test them on our own commutes, hikes, and daily errands through wind, rain, and everything in between. If a zipper sticks or a seam lets water through, we know about it long before a customer would.

Comfort, adjustability, and sustainability guide every decision we make, from the fabrics we choose to how a jacket is packaged. We'd rather release fewer styles and get them right than chase every passing trend.

We're still growing, and we're glad you're here for it. If you ever have feedback on a jacket you've bought from us, good or bad, we want to hear it, it's exactly how we keep improving.
