Shipping & Secure Payments

Shipping Information

We ship across Norway and the rest of Europe. Orders are packed and dispatched within 1-2 business days of being placed.

Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days within Norway, and 5-10 business days for the rest of Europe. Express shipping is available at checkout for faster delivery.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email so you can follow it the whole way to your door. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and chosen shipping method.

Secure Payments

All payments on Rainy Days are processed through encrypted, PCI-compliant payment providers. We never store your full card details on our servers.

We accept major credit and debit cards, as well as popular local payment methods. Every transaction is protected with industry-standard SSL encryption from the moment you enter your details to the moment your payment is confirmed.

If you ever notice anything unusual about a charge from us, reach out through our Contact Us page and we'll help sort it out right away.
